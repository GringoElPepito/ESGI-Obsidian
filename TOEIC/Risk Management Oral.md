Lion Air Flight 610 crashed into the Java Sea on October 29, 2018, killing all 189 aboard; Ethiopian Airlines Flight 302 crashed near Addis Ababa on March 10, 2019, killing all 157 — 346 dead combined. 
MIT Sloan Cause: MCAS, an automated anti-stall system relying on a single angle-of-attack sensor. When that sensor fed bad data, MCAS repeatedly forced the nose down in a way crews weren't trained to counter. 
Medium Preventable? Yes. Boeing's own engineers and test pilots knew about the MCAS problem before the crashes but withheld it from the FAA, airlines, and pilots, and the FAA had privately estimated MCAS could cause up to 15 crashes over 30 years back in December 2018 — before the second crash.
